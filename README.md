# Ecommerce Profit Intelligence: Margin Drivers and Value Leakage.  

## Project Overview

Ecommerce Profitability & Value Leakage Analysis is a Power BI analytics project that transforms ecommerce order, customer, and operational data into actionable insights on profitability, margin drivers, and lost revenue for a multi-channel European online retail business.  

The project analyzes sales performance, contribution margin, product and promotion effectiveness, customer segments, acquisition channels, returns, fulfillment, and inventory availability through three interactive dashboards: Executive Overview, Profitability Drivers, and Returns, Fulfillment & Inventory.  

The analysis combines DAX, data modeling, year-over-year KPI development, customer and product segmentation, and interactive data visualization to help leadership understand whether growth is translating into profit, which products, channels, and customers create the most value, and where returns, delivery issues, and stock-outs are eroding margin.
